Lost your code?

Enter the email address used at purchase. If a pack is associated with it, the office will re-send the claim code(s) to that address. No new code is minted, and no reply is given as to whether an address is on file.

How is my code stored?

Only in this browser, and only after you enter it. It is never written to an account, because Orphograph keeps no accounts for packs — the code itself is the instrument. Clearing your browser data removes it; recover it above with the email you bought with.

Do credits expire?

No. Anchors on a pack remain available until they are spent, however long that takes.

Can I anchor from anywhere?

Yes. The code is a bearer instrument: enter it on any device to draw from the same pack. Treat it like a key — anyone holding it can spend the remaining anchors.

I have not bought a pack yet.

I am on a Standing Order, not a pack.

A subscription keeps a receipt vault instead of a claim code. Sign in with your subscription email to reach it, or return to account access to choose your door.
