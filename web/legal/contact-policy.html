Legal · Contact and Persona

Contact and persona policy.

This document is a statement of the office's posture and is intended to be read by counsel where relevant. Nothing on this page constitutes legal advice.

1 · Authoritative channels

The office communicates officially through two addresses and two addresses only: hello@orphograph.com for general correspondence and legal@orphograph.com for matters of a legal character. Correspondence received through any other medium, including direct messages on social platforms, third-party messaging services, or unaddressed contact forms hosted by other parties, is not binding upon the office unless and until cross-confirmed through one of the two authoritative addresses above.

2 · Founder persona

The operator of the office writes under the persona "Orphograph's Founder" or, in formal correspondence, "the office". The legal name of the operator is held privately and is not a routine element of customer-facing correspondence. Correspondence that requires legal identification of the operator, including matters of service of process, regulatory inquiry, or formal contractual execution, is routed through legal@orphograph.com and may be answered by counsel of record on behalf of the operator.

3 · Press inquiries

Press inquiries are accepted at press@orphograph.com. The office reserves the right to respond, to decline to respond, or to refer the inquiry to another forum, at its discretion. Statements purported to be attributable to the office should be verified through press@orphograph.com prior to publication. The office does not regard statements made by other parties as binding upon it absent such verification.

4 · Impersonation

Communications purporting to originate from the office but not originating from an address at the @orphograph.com domain should not be treated as authoritative. The office advises recipients to disregard such communications and to confirm any matter of consequence through the authoritative channels above. Suspected impersonation should be reported to legal@orphograph.com, with the message in question preserved in its full original form, including headers, to the extent the reporter is able.

5 · Service of process

Formal legal service of process upon the office should be addressed in the first instance to legal@orphograph.com, accompanied by a request for the office's address of record. The address of record will be supplied promptly to a verified requester. The office does not waive any defect in service by reason of the foregoing, and reserves all rights as to the manner, sufficiency, and timeliness of service under the rules of the relevant forum.

6 · Right of publicity

The office does not authorise the use of its name, its seal, or any associated mark in any commercial endorsement, sponsorship, testimonial, or affiliated representation, save by express written authorisation issued by legal@orphograph.com. The founder's persona, voice, name, and image are not licensed for commercial use. Inadvertent or assumed authorisation is not a defence. Editorial, comparative, and academic reference consistent with the Trademark Notice is unaffected by this section.
