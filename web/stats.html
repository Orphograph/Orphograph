    What this page is not

    This page exists to show that the service is live, that anchors are being produced, and that the public infrastructure we depend on (OpenTimestamps calendars, the BTC price oracle) is reachable.

    It deliberately does not show: customer counts, customer emails, filenames, IPs, wallet balances, or any single receipt ID. Those would betray the buyers who anchored with us. Counts only — by design.
