Webhooks

Subscribers receive HMAC-signed POST notifications on anchor.created and anchor.btc_pinned events, and on subscription.renewed at each billing cycle rollover. Each delivery carries an X-Orpho-Signature header in the format t=<unix>,v1=<hmac-sha256-hex> (Stripe-compatible). Verify the signature with the secret issued at registration before trusting the payload.
