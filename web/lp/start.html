What the receipt is — and isn't

It proves

A file with this exact fingerprint existed by a specific Bitcoin block. Verifiable by anyone, against the public chain, without trusting us.

It does not claim

Authorship, ownership, originality, or that any court accepts it. Honest proof-of-existence in time — nothing more. That line is the whole point.
