Picking the right one for you

If you're comfortable in a terminal

Use OpenTimestamps directly. It's free, open, and Bitcoin-anchored — same protocol that powers Orphograph. pip install opentimestamps-client && ots stamp file.jpg is your full workflow. We exist to wrap this for people who don't live in pip-install land.

If you publish on WordPress

WordProof's WordPress plugin is built for you. €10/mo, integrates into your CMS. Different blockchain (theirs, not Bitcoin) but the same core idea.

If you're an EU enterprise with legal-evidence needs

OriginStamp pivoted B2B in May 2025 and now offers qualified eIDAS timestamps with full EU regulatory backing. CHF 25k setup is steep but if your use case is litigation, it's the only option in this lineup that legally clears that bar.

If you need camera-attestation provenance at the moment of capture

Truepic partners with camera OEMs (Sony, Leica, Nikon) for C2PA-conformant signed manifests embedded into the image at capture time. Enterprise contracts. Different shape from us — they prove WHO took the photo at capture; we prove WHEN the file existed.

If you're a working photographer who wants the date locked to Bitcoin without installing Python

That's us. $7 for a 10-pack of anchors, open-source verifier so your proofs outlive us, no upload, no account required for the free tier. Built solo, audited solo, written so the entire codebase fits in an afternoon read.

What we explicitly don't do

  • Court admissibility. We're not an eIDAS qualified TSA. For litigation, see OriginStamp or a qualified attorney.
  • Capture-time provenance. We anchor any file's hash; we don't sign it at the moment your camera shutter fires. (Roadmapped as "Orphograph Capture" — not yet built.)
  • Authorship proof. Anyone with your file can anchor it. The temporal moat is "anchor before you publish."
  • NFTs, tokens, or crypto-speculation. Bitcoin is the public ledger we anchor to; we don't mint anything, sell anything, or speculate on anything.

The trust thesis

Orphograph isn't the most powerful option in this list. It's the smallest, most reviewable, and most explicitly bounded. Six thousand lines of stdlib Python, three closed audits, open-source verifier so your receipts work without us. We do one thing — timestamp files cheaply — and we tell you exactly what it isn't.

Read our verifier (~100 lines) in five minutes. If it stops being trustworthy, you stop trusting us — your receipts still work.
