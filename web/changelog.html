Register of Revisions

A record of revisions to this office.

What follows is the chronological register of revisions to the instruments, notices, and published references of this office. Entries describe what an account-holder or visitor may newly observe; matters internal to the operation of the office are not enumerated.

18 May 2026

The address bar of every page now presents an institutional summary strip to signed-in account-holders, displaying the current plan, the count of anchors recorded, the days remaining to the next renewal, and a sign-out instrument.

A standalone independent verifier is published: a single hypertext document that confirms a receipt against the chain without reference to this office, and which may be retained and operated entirely apart from any server controlled by Orphograph.

Subscribers may register a webhook against their account; thereafter, a signed delivery is issued on every anchor recorded and on every Bitcoin commitment, addressed to a location of the subscriber's choosing.

Every receipt notice is now accompanied by a one-page printable instrument, formatted for filing.

The frequently asked questions are now set forth in formal address, with each answer phrased to the standard observed in correspondence from this office.

Anchoring from iOS is described in published form, accompanied by installation steps for the share-sheet shortcut by which a file may be submitted from a handheld device.

17 May 2026

A separate Writers cohort is recognised by this office. The price schedule has been revised to reflect a smaller Pack suited to a single manuscript.

16 May 2026

Orphograph is opened to the public at orphograph.com. The first anchor is registered. The source of the instrument is published under the MIT licence at github.com/Orphograph/Orphograph.

Subsequent revisions to this register are themselves anchored. Correspondence concerning a particular entry may be addressed to hello@orphograph.com.
