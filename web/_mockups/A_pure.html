— Terms of the Instrument —

What is recorded

The SHA-256 fingerprint of your file, the moment of submission, and the Bitcoin block whose proof-of-work attests it. Nothing else.

What is not recorded

The file's bytes, name, author, location of origin, or any metadata you do not voluntarily attach. No surveillance signal is produced.

What it proves

Existence at or before a specific Bitcoin block. This is the empirical claim — falsifiable by anyone, requiring no trust in this office.

What it does not claim

Authorship, ownership, legality, or court admissibility. Those are determined by other instruments and jurisdictions; this is the upstream fact only.
