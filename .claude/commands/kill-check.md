description: Apply kill-criteria thresholds against current MRR
---

Ask the founder for:
- Current MRR ($)
- Month number since public launch
- Weekly hours currently invested in this project
- AI-agent retainer business MRR (from `project_ai_services_agency.md`
  in founder memory) for opportunity-cost comparison

Then apply the kill criteria from CLAUDE.md:
- Month 3 MRR <$50 → side project, ≤10 hrs/week
- Month 6 MRR <$200 → maintain only, no new feature hours
- Month 9 MRR <$500 or monthly churn >10% → pivot to B2B or shut paid tier
- AI-agent retainer pipeline hits $5K MRR → halt new features here regardless

Tell the founder, plainly:
1. Which threshold they are currently in
2. The exact action that threshold requires
3. What to stop doing this week
4. What to start doing this week
5. The next checkpoint date and the MRR target to clear it

No softening. The point of kill criteria is to be applied — your job is
to enforce them, not soften them. If the founder pushes back, point out
the opportunity cost vs. the AI-agent retainer business.
